I'd divide PayPilot into three levels.

Phase 1 — Essential MVP
Must have

Dashboard with invoices and payment statuses.

Create an invoice using natural language.

Review and edit AI-generated invoice details.

Generate a PayPal sandbox checkout.

Complete and verify a test payment.

Show the updated invoice status.

Phase 2 — AI differentiation
High value

AI payment assistant with access to relevant invoice records.

Automatic identification of overdue invoices.

Payment reminder drafting.

An activity timeline showing what the AI suggested and what the user approved.

Clear validation and approval rules for payment actions.

Phase 3 — Polish
Optional

Search and filter invoices.

Revenue and payment analytics.

Responsive mobile layout.

Demo data, onboarding and a polished landing page.

A clear AI activity log and helpful error states.
